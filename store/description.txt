Vompt is a teleprompter that listens. Read your script at your own pace and the screen follows your voice: the word you're on lights up, and the current line stays on the reading mark. Skip a line, go back, or ad-lib, and Vompt catches up after a few words.

When the take ends, you see how it went.

HOW YOUR TAKE WENT
• Pace in words a minute, compared with a comfortable 130 to 165
• Every "um", "uh", "like" and "you know", counted and broken down
• Sentences you skipped, quoted so you can find them
• Words that didn't match the script, restarts, and long pauses
• Short coaching notes from Apple Intelligence on supported devices

PRACTICE THAT ADDS UP
Every take is saved with its script. Watch your fillers a minute fall from one take to the next.

WRITE BETTER SCRIPTS
Assist uses Apple Intelligence, on your iPhone, to:
• Make a script sound spoken instead of written
• Tighten it without losing a point
• Fit it to 30 seconds, a minute, or any length
• Fix spelling and grammar
• Suggest opening lines that hook viewers
• Draft a script from your bullet points

RUN IT FROM YOUR COMPUTER
Turn on Vompt Studio and open the link on a laptop on the same Wi-Fi. Write with a real keyboard, use Assist, send a script to the prompter, and start, pause and watch the take live, with the phone on its stand.

MADE FOR REAL SETUPS
• Mirror mode for beam-splitter teleprompter glass
• Adjustable text size, centered or left-aligned lines
• Long-press any word to continue from there
• Works with AirPods and wired microphones
• Keeps the screen awake while you read

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Speech is recognized on the device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, there's no account, and there are no ads or tracking. Your scripts stay on your iPhone.

Vompt is free and its source code is public.

Filler counting for "um" and "uh" and the fastest tracking require iOS 26 or later. Assist and coaching notes require a device with Apple Intelligence.

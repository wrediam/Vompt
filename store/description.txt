Your voice runs the prompter. Read your script at your own pace and Vompt follows along: the word you're on lights up, and the current line stays on the reading mark. Skip a line, go back, or ad-lib, and it catches up after a few words.

FOLLOWS YOUR VOICE
Vompt listens with Apple's on-device speech recognition and tracks your place word by word, about half a second behind you. No scroll speed to set, no foot pedal, no one running the prompter for you.

RUN IT FROM YOUR BROWSER
Turn on Vompt Studio and open the link on a laptop or tablet on the same Wi-Fi. Your iPhone serves the page itself, so nothing goes through the internet.
• Write and edit scripts with a real keyboard
• Send a script to the prompter with one click
• Start, pause, change text size and mirror the display
• Watch a live preview with the current word, time, pace and filler count
• See the take summary the moment you finish
A private link, new every session, keeps your scripts away from others on shared Wi-Fi.

ON-DEVICE AI
Assist uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, in the app and in Studio:
• Make a script sound spoken instead of written
• Tighten it without losing a point
• Fit it to 30 seconds, a minute, or any length
• Fix spelling and grammar
• Suggest opening lines that hook viewers
• Draft a script from your bullet points
Before you use a rewrite, Assist shows exactly what it changed: added words highlighted, removed words struck through. After each take, it also writes short coaching notes from your results. Your script never leaves the device.

EVERY DRAFT, KEPT
Vompt saves a version as you edit and every time Assist rewrites your script. Browse them on a timeline, compare any version with your script now, word by word, and bring it back in a tap. Restoring saves your current text first, so nothing is ever lost.

HOW YOUR TAKE WENT
• Pace in words a minute, compared with a comfortable 130 to 165
• Every "um", "uh", "like" and "you know", counted and broken down
• Sentences you skipped, quoted so you can find them
• Words that didn't match the script, restarts, and long pauses
Every take is saved with its script, so you can watch your fillers a minute fall.

MADE FOR REAL SETUPS
• Mirror mode for beam-splitter teleprompter glass
• Adjustable text size, centered or left-aligned lines
• Long-press any word to continue from there
• Works with AirPods and wired microphones
• Keeps the screen awake while you read

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Speech is recognized on the device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, there's no account, and there are no ads or tracking.

Vompt is free and its source code is public.

Filler counting for "um" and "uh" and the fastest tracking require iOS 26 or later. Assist and coaching notes require a device with Apple Intelligence.

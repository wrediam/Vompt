# Vompt privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Vompt does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that track you.

## Microphone and speech

While the prompter is listening, Vompt uses the microphone to follow your place in the script and to count filler words. Speech is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is processed in memory and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The transcript is used only during the take and is discarded afterwards.

## Your scripts and practice history

Scripts and the results of your practice takes (time, pace, filler counts, and so on) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are included in your device backups as with any app. Deleting a script deletes its practice history; deleting the app deletes everything.

## Apple Intelligence

The Assist tools use Apple's on-device language model. Your script is processed on the device and is not sent to Vompt or any server.

## Web remote (Vompt Studio)

If you turn on the web remote, the app serves a page on your local Wi-Fi network so you can edit scripts and control the prompter from a computer. It is reachable only on that network and only with the private link shown on the device, which changes each time the remote starts. Nothing is sent over the internet.

## Children

Vompt does not collect data from anyone, including children.
